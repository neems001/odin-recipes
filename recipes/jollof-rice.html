jollof rice recipe

Description

Jollof rice is an african food made with rice, tomato, onions and pepper steamed together to make a colourful and rich tasting dish

Ingredients

  • rice
  • Tomato/tomato paste
  • Onions
  • salt and seasonings
  • water
  • oil
  • meat stock(optional)

Steps

  1. Put water in a pot and leave to boil
  2. Add oil and your blended tomato, pepper, and onions
  3. After a few minutes add your seasonings and meat stock(if in use)
  4. Pour your washed rice into the pot
  5. cook till done and enjoy with your favorite protein
